Rates use only attempts that reached a verdict as the denominator; attempts without one are counted in Report 1. Correct rate = correct ÷ (correct + wrong identity + rejected) over genuine probes. FRR = rejected ÷ the same. FAR = non-enrolled inputs identified ÷ non-enrolled inputs with a verdict. Precision = correct ÷ all identifications; recall = correct ÷ genuine attempts with a verdict; F1 = harmonic mean. The match score is a cosine similarity, not a calibrated probability.
